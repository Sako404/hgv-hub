# Project identity

The software is licensed under **AGPL-3.0-or-later**. That licence governs the
code. It does not govern the project's identity, and the two are deliberately
separate concerns.

**No trademark is registered.** Nothing here claims registration, and the ® symbol
is not used. This is a practical statement about not misleading people, not a
legal instrument.

## What you may do

Everything the AGPL grants: use, study, modify, redistribute, self-host, and
fork — including commercially, on the licence's terms. You do not need
permission, and nothing below restricts those rights.

## What this asks

**Do not present a fork as the official HGV HUB project.**

If you distribute a modified version, make it clear it is yours:

- do not describe your fork as the official HGV HUB, or as an official release;
- do not imply endorsement by, or affiliation with, the upstream project;
- if confusion is likely — a public deployment, a published package, an app
  listing — use your own name for it.

Saying "based on HGV HUB" or "a fork of HGV HUB" is accurate, welcome, and
exactly the sort of attribution the AGPL and this document both expect.

## Official upstream

    https://github.com/Sako404/hgv-hub

That repository is the only official source. Anything else is a fork, which is
fine — it just is not this project.
